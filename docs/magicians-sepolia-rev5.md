Dispute registry `0x01091Fd4b0bEEe3216d20973aF0A79AbAFF6CBcb` (one address for the whole `IADRFDisputeRegistry`, over six modules), policy registry `0x90FdC8B9F46537429400E4318023fc4D32FdCec3`, escrow `0x5B1d6B799c4BE0Baf2BfEF08D47C57627bFCe2D6`, attestor rule `0xAcB4f7c7842DE670f3e3521dc8E9503059db31Cb`, bridge `0xA6976e75F511d7042884507e28413C36384dEA76` over the same live ERC-8436 registry as before (`0x3bE57E74…`). The revision-3 and revision-4 deployments stay where they are; this is a third deployment, not a replacement.

Besides the two flows of the earlier runs, which ran unchanged (the double charge, case `0xf8ec53d8…`, verified and refunded from the held settlement; the delivery-quality claim, case `0x74f1fac3…`, judged through ERC-8436 issue `0xa3ea4ac9…` and refunded), the run carries the two scenarios of posts 10 to 13:

- **The procedure in force at the event.** Call 3 was delivered at 15:11:24 UTC under the first binding; the escrow revoked that binding at 15:11:48 and bound the amended policy (version 2 of the family: a 2-day filing window, a 10-minute evidence window) at 15:12:00; call 4 was delivered at 15:12:36 under amended terms. The earlier delivery was filed under the revoked binding after the revocation and admitted, since it occurred while that binding was in force, with version 1's 30-minute evidence window; the later delivery was filed under the amended binding with version 2's 10 minutes. Filing the earlier delivery under the amended binding, or the later one under the revoked binding, is refused with `BindingNotInForce`, and the earlier delivery under the amended terms with `NormNotApplicable` — checked against the deployed contracts during the run and again by `eth_call` afterwards. The earlier case (`0x35b8beff…`) was decided, finalized and refunded by the escrow after it had moved to the amended policy: the ruling names the revoked binding, and the escrow enacts rulings under any binding it registered.
- **Pending versus terminal.** On the earlier case, after one attestation, a resolution by an unrelated account reverted with `WindowOpen("evidence")`; the second attestation formed the quorum, a third one for the other value was refused with `Rule_QuorumReached`, and the finding recorded was the quorum's `FALSE` (`signed | evidenced`). On the later case (`0x7222502b…`) a single attestation stayed below the quorum: the early resolution reverted in the same way, the window closed at 15:23:12, an attestation after it was refused with `Rule_WindowClosed`, and the resolution after the window recorded `UNDETERMINED` with reason `METHOD_UNDETERMINED` — Final as `NoDecision`, no effect, the fallback the claim named, call 4's settlement still held. The tallies read back from the rule are 0–2 and 0–1.

The deploy phase registered both versions of the ADRF policy, reused the ERC-8436 fast-track policy registered by the first run and needed no funding transfers, hence 17 transactions there. The largest creations landed within 0.6% of the pre-flight estimates: the escrow at 14.49 million gas (86% of the 16,777,216-gas cap), the filing module at 14.18 million (85%); the revised attestor rule costs 6.30 million for its 3,940 bytes; the eight units whose bytecode did not change cost within a few dozen gas of what they cost in revision 4 (only their constructor arguments differ).

The record — addresses, identifiers, every transaction with block, gas and status, the state read back from the chain, the identifiers and exclusivity keys recomputed and matched, the seven local checks and the four re-checked by `eth_call` — is `docs/sepolia-evidence-rev5.md` in the repository, with `deployments/sepolia-rev5.json` and `deployments/sepolia-rev5.transactions.json`.
