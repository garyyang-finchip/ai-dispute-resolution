# Deployments

`sepolia-rev5.json` is the record of the Sepolia run of 2026-11-17 with the revision-5 contracts (the policy registry, the six modules, the dispute registry, the methods, the bridge, the escrow, the live ERC-8436 registry, the identifiers of the three policies, the panel, the two bindings, the four cases and the ERC-8436 issue, the actors, and the outcomes of the run's seven local checks) and `sepolia-rev5.transactions.json` its 63 transactions with hash, block, status, gas used and effective gas price, collected from the broadcast files by `scripts/collect-sepolia-record.py`; the evidence document is `docs/sepolia-evidence-rev5.md`. `sepolia-rev4.json` / `sepolia-rev4.transactions.json` (45 transactions, `docs/sepolia-evidence-rev4.md`) and `sepolia.json` / `sepolia.transactions.json` (46 transactions, `docs/sepolia-evidence.md`) are the records of the revision-4 and revision-3 runs of 2026-10-29; their contracts stay deployed. The Anvil rehearsal of `script/SepoliaEvidence.s.sol` writes `31337.json` here (ignored by git); a run writes `<chainId>.json` and `scripts/sepolia-run.sh record` copies it to `$RECORD.json` (default `sepolia-rev6`, the record the revision-6 run will write). `scripts/sepolia-readback.sh` and `scripts/sepolia-verify.sh` read the record named by `RECORD`.
