# Deployments

`sepolia-rev4.json` is the record of the Sepolia run of 2026-10-29 with the revision-4 contracts (the policy registry, the six modules, the dispute registry, the methods, the bridge, the escrow, the live ERC-8436 registry, the identifiers of the two policies, the panel, the binding, the two cases and the ERC-8436 issue) and `sepolia-rev4.transactions.json` its 45 transactions with hash, block, status, gas used and effective gas price, collected from the broadcast files by `scripts/collect-sepolia-record.py`; the evidence document is `docs/sepolia-evidence-rev4.md`. `sepolia.json` and `sepolia.transactions.json` (46 transactions) are the same record for the revision-3 run of the same day, documented in `docs/sepolia-evidence.md`; its contracts stay deployed. The Anvil rehearsal of `script/SepoliaEvidence.s.sol` writes `31337.json` here (ignored by git); a run writes `<chainId>.json` and `scripts/sepolia-run.sh record` copies it to `$RECORD.json` (default `sepolia-rev4`). `scripts/sepolia-readback.sh` and `scripts/sepolia-verify.sh` read the record named by `RECORD`.
